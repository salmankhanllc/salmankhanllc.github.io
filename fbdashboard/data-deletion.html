Data Deletion — FB Dashboard

FB Dashboard by Salman Khan · Last updated: 27 November 2026

Where your data lives

FB Dashboard stores your data only on your own device — in the app's private storage on Android, or in a data folder next to the app on desktop. The developer holds no copy of your accounts, access tokens, posts or settings on any server, so there is no server-side copy for us to delete.

Delete everything, inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open FB Dashboard and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete all my data.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

This immediately wipes your connected accounts and access tokens, scheduled posts, notifications log and settings from the device. Your license activation is kept, as it is a purchase right rather than personal data.

Other ways to remove data

  • Remove one account: open the Accounts page in the app and remove that account — its token and data are deleted from the device.
  • Uninstall the app: uninstalling FB Dashboard on Android also removes the app's private storage. On desktop, deleting the app's data folder removes the stored data.
  • Revoke Facebook access: you can also remove FB Dashboard's access from your Facebook account's Settings → Apps and Websites.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, you can send a data deletion request to Salman Khan at salmankhanllc90009@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and the name of the Facebook account you connected. Because your data lives only on your own device, we will reply with confirmation and, if needed, step-by-step help to delete it in the app — in-app deletion is immediate and complete.
