What FB Dashboard does

Built for people who manage many Pages and want the daily work to take minutes, not hours.

Many Pages, many accounts

Connect more than one Facebook account and manage all of their Pages — 10, 20, even 50 Pages — from a single dashboard.

Auto Post

Fill the posting queue once, set up to 3 daily times, and the app publishes on its own — no daily effort from you.

Scheduler

Write posts now and schedule them for later. Each scheduled post publishes with the account that created it.

Comments management

See the comments on every post and reply, like, hide or delete them — without opening Facebook again and again.

Media library

Browse the photos, videos and reels of your Pages, publish new posts and stories, and delete media you no longer need.

Analytics

Views, engagement and follower growth for each Page — daily, weekly or the last 28 days — in one clean view.

Updates inside the app

When a new version is released, your app offers it on its own Updates page. No files to hunt for, no reinstalling by hand.

Simple license activation

One personal license key activates your copy. Activation is checked on your own device — the app works without any account system of ours.
