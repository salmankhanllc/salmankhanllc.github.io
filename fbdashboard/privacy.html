Privacy Policy — FB Dashboard

FB Dashboard by Salman Khan · Last updated: 27 November 2026

FB Dashboard is built around one simple rule: your Facebook data stays on your own device. The app has no account system of ours, no analytics, no tracking and no cloud server that your data passes through.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app saves lives in the app's own storage on the device you are using — on Android, in the app's private internal storage; on desktop, in a data folder next to the app:

  • The Facebook accounts you add, including their access tokens and profile pictures.
  • Your scheduled posts (message, Page and time) so they can publish when they are due.
  • Your settings (such as the theme), your notifications log and your default Page.
  • Your 2FA authenticator secrets, if you add entries in Settings → 2FA Authenticator.
  • Your license key and, if you set one, your app-lock PIN — the PIN is only ever stored as a salted hash, never as the PIN itself.

What the app talks to

The only service the app contacts is Facebook's official Graph API (graph.facebook.com), using the access token of the account you connected through Facebook's official login. That is how your Pages, posts, media, inbox and analytics load, and how posts are published to your own Pages. Your token is sent to Facebook only — it is never sent to the developer of this app or to any other third party.

What is never collected

The developer receives nothing from your app: no tokens, no Page data, no usage statistics, no crash reports. License keys are verified offline, on the device itself — activating the app does not phone home either.

Backups are your responsibility

If you export a backup from Settings or the Accounts page, that file contains your access tokens — treat it exactly like a password and keep it somewhere private. Anyone holding the file can use your accounts until the tokens expire.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything the app has stored at any time: go to Settings → Delete all my data in the app, type DELETE and confirm. That wipes your accounts, tokens, scheduled posts, notifications and settings from the device. (Your license activation is kept — it is a purchase right, not personal data.) Removing a single account is possible at any time from the Accounts page. See also our Data Deletion page.
